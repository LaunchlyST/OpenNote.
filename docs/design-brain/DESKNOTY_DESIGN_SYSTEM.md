# Design system — Concept A "The Threshold" (proposal)

Principles (from RESEARCH): one lit focal object per screen; product is the hero;
price strong and beside the action; campaign block between rows; the hero is an object
(a frame) inside the page.

## World (intro)
- Material: warm limestone / plaster monoliths, matte; fog the colour of dusk paper
  (#d8cfc2 → #6e655c). Light: one warm raking key light + cool rim. No neon, no purple.
- The door: two thick slabs, engraved store name, a hairline seam that glows warm white.

## Store
- Palette: bone #f3efe7, plaster #e6dfd3, ink #141312, mute #6f6a62, line #e3ddd2;
  accent = seller theme colour (`--ac`), used only for sale prices and the discount mark.
- Type: Instrument Serif (display, tight -0.03em), Geist (UI/body),
  JetBrains Mono (small caps labels, 11–12 px, +0.12em).
- Layout: 1280 max content; 24 px side gutter (16 on phones); 8-pt rhythm.
- Hero = the arch frame: store name huge, real bio, CTA "Shop the collection",
  fanned real covers inside the frame.
- Product rhythm: first product as a **feature row** (big art + buy box), then a
  2–3-column grid, a **campaign block** (real discount only) between rows, Next drop panel.
- Card: 4:5 art (cover, top-aligned), discount mark top-left, format chip top-right,
  title (serif 28), 2-line description, price right of title, full-width ink CTA.
- PDP: gallery left / sticky buy box right; buy box = kicker, title, rating (real),
  price + discount, CTA, "What you get" ticks, description.
- Radii: 18–22 media, 999 buttons. Shadows: soft, one direction (light from top-left).
